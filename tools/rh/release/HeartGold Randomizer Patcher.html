HeartGold Randomizer Patcher

Turns your own Pokémon HeartGold (USA) ROM into the HeartGold Expansion Randomizer. Everything happens on this computer: nothing is uploaded, and it works without internet.

1. Your HeartGold ROM

A clean Pokémon HeartGold (USA) v1.0 .nds file (not already patched).

2. The patch

The HeartGold Expansion Randomizer vX.Y.Z.xdelta file from the Releases page.

Keep your original ROM: every new version of the patch needs the clean game again.
